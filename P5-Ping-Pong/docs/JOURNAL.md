- 2026-10-07 — I asked for the paddle on screen to turn when I turn the hub in my hand. - 2026-10-07 — "Think about the delay from everything": the hub stamps samples on arrival (~40 ms after the hand did it), the camera pipeline is 100 ms or more behind the hand, the screen shows a frame ~50 ms after it is drawn, and the speakers and motors add their own. `pingpong/latency.py` models each stage (only the camera's lag is measured; the rest are estimates with a basis, all knobs: `config_local.json` or `--set latency.display_s=0.08`) and the game places every effect where it will be PERCEIVED: the ball and the computer's paddle (deterministic flights) are drawn ahead by the display's delay, my hand is extrapolated across its pipeline (capped, damped), the swing is dated by when I did it (peak + stroke - hub delay), and a hit's thump and sound are sent early by the motors' and speakers' delays so they arrive with the picture. A pause freezes the picture (and an old bug appeared with it: after a pause the judge held the unshifted copy of the ball's flight).

- 2026-10-07 — A correction to my own last commit: the "delay of everything" change drew my hand where its speed said it would be when the frame is seen, and I never measured whether that was better. - 2026-10-07 — "Train the model to be smoother and more accurate": what can be trained on one player's own recordings is smaller than it sounds.
